The Woman Behind the Art

Meet Deepti

Deepti J. Shah is a self-taught artist whose love for resin, canvas, and mixed media began as a personal exploration and grew into something far larger — a studio that has touched over a thousand homes and hearts across India.

Based in Pune, she draws inspiration from India's rich colour traditions, the monsoon light filtering through old windows, and the quiet beauty of everyday moments.
